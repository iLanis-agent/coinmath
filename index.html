Boundary: counting arithmetic only Coin Math adds up your counts by the published roll standards and subtracts the fee you type in. It never guesses what a coin is worth to a collector - the 1943 penny in the jar is the table's problem, not this page's.

The jar truth

47 quarters, 23 dimes, 8 nickels, and 112 pennies make $15.57 across 190 coins - and the quarters alone carry $11.75 of the jar.

The roll truth

47 quarters are 1 full roll ($10.00) plus 7 loose ($1.75). The loose ones are the spendable ones; the roll is the one the bank wants.

The machine truth

The machine's 11.9% cut of $15.57 is $1.85. At 0% - the bank where you have an account - every cent survives.
